How to play

Step 1 — Tap Play

  1. Tap the big Play button above. The game opens right in your browser — nothing to install, nothing to download.
  2. It plays on phones, tablets and computers. Turn your phone sideways for the full table.

Step 2 — Roll the dice

  • Street craps, Hustle City rules: $100 minimum bet, even money, $2,000 in chips to start. Roll a 7 or 11 on the come-out and you win; 2, 3 or 12 and the table takes it; any other number becomes your point — hit it again before a 7 shows and you're paid.

  • Play chips only. No real money is ever bet, won or lost in this game — it's a game, plain and simple.
