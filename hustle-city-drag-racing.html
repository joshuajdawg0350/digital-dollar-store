How to play

Step 1 — Open it

  1. Tap Play Hustle City Drag Racing above (after checkout, your thank-you page brings you to the same place). It opens right in your browser — nothing to install, nothing to download.
  2. Bookmark it or add it to your home screen: that address is its permanent home.

Step 2 — Race

  • Do your burnout, stage up, and watch the Christmas tree. Launch on green — jump early and you red-light; sleep and the race is gone.
  • As the tach needle sweeps, tap SHIFT as it crosses the green zone. Shift too late and you flirt with the redline — hold it there and you'll blow the motor.
  • Hit NITRO for the boost when you need the top end.
  • Your gauges sit right above the NITRO button and the tree sits top-center — everything you need is on one screen, no scrolling.

Step 3 — Build in the Garage

  • Wins pay cash. Spend it in the Garage — its own screen, one tap from anywhere — on the 87-step upgrade path from STOCK to MAX.
  • Save your game. Tap SAVE GAME in the Garage and your car, cash and record are kept between visits. No save, and you start over from stock — that's the rule, plain and simple.

Step 4 — Beat Scotty

  • Sixteen rivals stand between you and the top. The last one is Scotty — the record holder, and the wall. In our measured testing, good driving beat the rest of the field about 9 times out of 10, but Scotty at MAX build fell only to perfect runs. Bring your best.
