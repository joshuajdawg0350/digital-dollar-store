How to use it

Step 1 — Open it

  1. Tap Open Draw Pad above (after checkout, your thank-you page brings you to the same place). It opens right in your browser — nothing to install, nothing to download.
  2. Bookmark it or add it to your home screen: that address is its permanent home.

Step 2 — Pick a color and a brush

  • Tap one of the eight color circles at the top. Electric blue is picked first.
  • Tap a brush size — the three buttons with dots: small, medium, large.

Step 3 — Draw

  • Drag your finger (or mouse) on the dark sheet and it draws. That's the whole trick.
  • Eraser rubs lines back out. ↩ Undo takes back your last line. Clear wipes the whole page — it asks you first, so a slip can't wreck your work.
  • The ? button at the top explains all of it again, right inside the app.

Step 4 — It saves itself

  • Your drawing saves itself on this device as you draw. Close the page and come back later — it's still there, exactly how you left it.
  • The counter at the bottom tells you how many lines you've drawn, and whether the save worked.
