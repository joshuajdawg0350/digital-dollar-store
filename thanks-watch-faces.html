What a photo watch face is — honest and plain

Your watch shows its own clock: the time, the date, and anything else your watch puts on screen. These pictures go under that clock, like wallpaper for your watch. We make the art. Your watch makes the clock. That is how photo faces work on every watch — Apple Watch, Galaxy Watch, and Wear OS watches like the Pixel Watch.

Put one on your watch

Step 1 — Get the pictures on your phone

  1. Tap the download button above and save the zip.
  2. Tap it (phone) or double-click it (computer) to open it.
  3. Save the pictures from the faces folder to your phone's Photos (iPhone) or Gallery (Android). The Files app (iPhone) or My Files / Files by Google (Android) opens zip files — nothing extra to buy.

Step 2 — Apple Watch

  1. Open one of the pictures in the Photos app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Create Watch Face.
  4. Tap Photos Face. If it asks where the time goes, pick the spot you like.
  5. Tap Add. Raise your wrist — your new face is on.

To change it later: open the Watch app, tap "My Faces", and pick any picture you saved.

Step 3 — Galaxy Watch (Samsung)

  1. Save the pictures to your phone's Gallery (Step 1).
  2. Open the Galaxy Wearable app on your phone.
  3. Tap Watch faces.
  4. Find My photo+ and tap it. It is under the Graphic faces.
  5. Tap Customize, pick a picture, then pick a clock style and color you can read easy.
  6. Tap Save. The face is now on your watch.

Step 4 — Wear OS (Pixel Watch and others)

  1. Save the pictures to your phone's Photos (Step 1).
  2. On the watch, press and hold the face you have now.
  3. Swipe and tap Add watch face, then pick Photos.
  4. Pick a picture, pick a clock style, and tap Set.

You can do the same from your watch's phone app (the Pixel Watch app or your watch maker's app): open Watch faces, pick Photos, and choose a picture.

Every picture in this pack was made with a calm spot near the top middle, so your clock sits on a quiet part of the art and stays easy to read. Round watch or square watch, both work — your watch crops the middle, and the important art is in the middle.
