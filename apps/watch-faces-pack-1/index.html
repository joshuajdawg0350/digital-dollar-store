How to put a face on your watch

  1. Tap "See it big" on the face you want, then press and hold the big picture and save it to your phone's Photos or Gallery.
  2. Apple Watch: open the picture in Photos, tap Share, then "Create Watch Face" — or in the Watch app, Face Gallery → Photos.
  3. Galaxy Watch / Wear OS: open the Galaxy Wearable or watch app, go to Watch faces → your photo option, and pick the picture you saved.
  4. Done. Your watch's clock shows over the picture, like it should.
