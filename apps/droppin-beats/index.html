Quick Split — pull a song apart

Import a song above, then split it into parts you can play, download, or chop in the Sample Lab. Honest note: this is a quick split, not AI. It uses the old studio tricks — sounds in the middle of a stereo mix (usually the lead vocal) can be turned down or pulled out, and low sounds can be separated from high ones. It works best on stereo mixes; on some songs the vocal will still peek through. Every part is labelled for what it really is.
