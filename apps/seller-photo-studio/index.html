Read first — what this studio will not do

  • No background removal. No AI anything. This studio does sizing, framing, light correction, spec checks, before/after pairs, and collages. It does not cut subjects out of photos or invent new backgrounds — a browser page cannot honestly do that; tools that promise it are cloud AI with subscriptions and credits.
  • Light correction cannot create detail. Upscaling a 400 px photo to 1,600 px makes a bigger file, not a sharper photo. This studio never upscales — the checklist flags too-small originals instead of faking them.
  • Specs are guidance, not a guarantee. Marketplace rules below were checked Oct 18, 2026. Marketplaces change rules without notice — confirm before a big upload.
  • Batch size: keep it to about 12 photos at a time on a phone. Very large batches are bounded by phone memory; if the page slows down, split the set.
  • Need a real AI cutout? That is Photoroom's job — its paid plans start at $12.99/mo, and its free tier is personal use only. This studio won't fake one.
