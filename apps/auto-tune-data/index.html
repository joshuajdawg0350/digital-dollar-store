    Tune Profiles

    Two tune files for this car. Whichever one is selected here is the one the Tune Advisor and the Shoot Sheet work against.

    NEVER SPRAY ON THIS FILE.

    Advanced timing + nitrous = engine damage. If nitrous is going in the car, the N₂O FILE goes in first. No exceptions.

    NA File — the Most Wanted Tune (DiabloSport inTune i3 Platinum 8345)
    • Published gains for THIS engine: 93-oct +15 hp / +20 lb-ft · 91-oct +13/+18 · 89-oct +10/+15.
    • Honest real-world expectation: ~8–15 crank hp. The real win is feel — throttle response — not peak numbers.
    • Throttle Booster: +20% (max) — the #1 owner want.
    • Speed limiter: removed / raised.
    • Fans: all settings −20°F with a 180°F thermostat (stock stat is 203°F) — LS 189 / MS 192 / HS 194°F.
    • WOT spark: tune to ZERO knock retard at WOT (>1° KR = pull timing). Sliders ±10°, bands 1k–3.8k / 4k–4.8k / 5k–7k. WOT fuel sliders ±20%, same bands.
    • Tire size calibration: 245/45R20 (~28.7″).
    • ESP / traction control: LEAVE ON — this car runs right with TC on.
    • Advisor targets on this file: cruise AFR 14.7, WOT AFR 12.5–13.0.
    ⚠️ 42RLE gotcha

    Shift firmness, autostick delete, TCM reset and axle-ratio changes are documented for the NAG1 5-speed only. This car has the 42RLE 4-speed — those items are N/A on 42RLE, do not chase them. This tune is engine-side only: spark, fuel, throttle, limiters, fans.

    N₂O File — how it’s built
    • Starts from STOCK timing, then retard vs stock WOT spark (ZEX tune-up sheet): −2° (50 shot) / −4° (75) / −6° (100).
    • AFR on spray target: 10.8–11.8 (general nitrous consensus, not 3.5-specific published data) — err rich. Any WOT cell leaner than 11.8 fails hard in the advisor.
    • 93 octane minimum; race fuel advised above a 75 shot.
    • Plugs: 1 step colder (50/75 shot), 1–2 steps colder (100 shot).
    • Spray window: ≥3,000 RPM, wide-open throttle only (window switch).
    • Advisor WOT flags on this file use the spray band, and the lean-on-spray hard warning fires for any WOT cell leaner than 11.8.

    Timing retard — general guidance for your tuner

    Per ZEX’s published tune-up sheet for this kind of setup. General information, not an instruction — discuss with your tuner and verify on a real dyno before spraying.

    Sample Data — SAMPLE, synthetic

    A built-in SAMPLE dataset made for this car: a 2008 Charger 3.5L V6 log with a mild cruise trim offset, one lean high-RPM wide-open cell, and one low-timing high-load cell — plus a SAMPLE dyno pull. Nothing here came from a real car; it exists so every screen works with zero hardware.

    What the SAMPLE log should show
    • A cruise fuel correction bigger than 5% (mild positive trims at low RPM).
    • A lean 5,500+ RPM wide-open cell (AFR ~13.8, trims adding ~+10%) that fires the nitrous safety warning.
    • A high-load cell around 3,500–4,500 RPM with ~6° timing advance that fires a timing flag.
