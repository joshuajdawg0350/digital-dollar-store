    About

    Pocket Player™ runs entirely on your phone, right in this page — it works offline once loaded. Sound chain: your file → 10-band EQ → bass → loudness (compressor + limiter, so loud stays clean instead of clipping) → your speaker, headphones, or Bluetooth (Bluetooth is simply your phone’s output route — pick it in your phone’s settings). The loudness reading on each song comes from a scan of the actual file on this device.
