Read this first — the honest limits

  • No AI auto-captions. Captions here are your own words, timed by tapping or nudging. Built-in speech recognition is inconsistent and often sends your audio to a cloud server, which would break this app's offline, no-upload promise — so it is left out on purpose.
  • Export is real-time. A 60-second clip takes about 60 seconds to export. Quality and bitrate follow your device and browser encoder. The exported file's true format is whatever your browser actually recorded, and it is labeled with that real format — never renamed to look like something else.
  • Checking what this browser can record…
  • A browser app has limits. It cannot post to TikTok, Reels, or Shorts for you, cannot use another app's music library, and cannot beat a native editor on 4K or long-form work. If you only make Reels and want a free native editor, Instagram's own Edits app is a solid free alternative.
  • Nothing is saved anywhere but here. No account, no cloud project, no sync. Your clip and captions never leave this device. If you close this page without exporting the SRT caption file, your timings are gone — so export the SRT early, not just at the end. There is an SRT button in the Captions step for exactly that.
  • Practical lane: short-form clips up to about 3 minutes. Longer or very high-resolution clips can run this phone out of memory while decoding audio or exporting. Shorter is smoother.
  • No camera or microphone permission is ever requested. The only thing this app asks for is the video file you pick.
