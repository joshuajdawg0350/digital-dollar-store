How to play

Tap a game above and it starts — everything runs right here in your browser, on your phone or computer. Nothing to download, nothing to install.

  • Chess & Checkers: tap one of your pieces, then tap where it should go. Legal spots light up.
  • Solitaire: tap a card to pick it up, tap where it goes. Double-tap a card to send it to a foundation pile. (Cards move by tapping — there is no dragging.)
  • Crosswords: tap a square, type the letter. Tap the same square again to switch between Across and Down.
  • Make it yours: every game has three looks (skins) — tap the skin button in the game to switch. Your pick is remembered.
  • Your progress is saved on this device automatically.

Plain truth: not every Solitaire deal can be won — that's how Klondike is, and always has been. If a deal beats you, deal a fresh one.
