How to use Create with J-Dawg

The basics — 4 steps:

  • 1. Pick a color — tap any color in the bottom bar. Tap "All Colors" for our full Electric Color Chart, or the rainbow square to mix any color.
  • 2. Pick a brush — tap a tool at the top: Pen, Pencil, Marker, Brush, Spray, Calligraphy…
  • 3. Draw — drag your finger (or mouse) on the picture.
  • 4. Save — tap the big blue SAVE PICTURE button at the top. The picture lands in your downloads. Your drawing also saves itself as you go — close the page and come back, it's still here.

Good to know:

  • ERASE EVERYTHING (top row, red) wipes the whole picture. It asks you to tap twice, so a slip can't wreck your work.
  • Mistakes? Undo and Redo are the first two buttons in the top row.
  • Smooth steadies shaky lines — slide it up for clean outlines.
  • Mirror draws both sides at once. Tap it again for 4-way kaleidoscope.
  • Fill (paint bucket) colors a whole area with one tap.
  • Dropper — tap it, then tap any spot in your picture to draw with that exact color.
  • Templates give you coloring pages, comic panels, graph paper and tracing sheets underneath your drawing.
  • Layers are clear sheets stacked up — draw on one without touching the others.
  • Zoom: pinch with two fingers, or use two fingers to slide the picture around.
  • No ads. No account. Nothing you draw ever leaves your device.
