The win-rate law — 100 seeded races per matchup

✓ PASS — 16 rivals × 4 builds × 3 driving lines, 100 races in every cell

  • GOOD driving vs the field (clean launches, shifts in the green) won at 0.897 / 0.887 / 0.859 / 0.867 across STOCK, 8-part, DRAG SPEC and MAX builds — good driving wins about 9 of 10 against matched rivals, at every level of build.
  • PERFECT driving won 1.000 — every single race — against all 16 rivals at all 4 builds, Scotty included: 6,400 seeded PERFECT races, never beaten once.

  • SLOPPY driving won 0.000 — every cell, every build. Mistakes lose. The game rewards driving, not luck.

The honest limits — read these before you buy

  • The shift cadence is proportional, not metronome-even. One shift rhythm runs through every build, with each gear taking a similar share of the run. That means at MAX build the whole run is 2.5 seconds, so the gears are short — gears 3 and 4 at the top end last about a quarter-second each (a 0.242-second floor in the tuning). It plays fast and smooth, but if you want every gear to last the same number of seconds at every build, that is not how this build works — the elapsed-time ladder above is what's guaranteed.
  • 5 of the 87 upgrade steps buy no time. Aero tier 2 and chute tiers 1–4 change nothing on the clock in the measured audit. The other 82 steps all improve the car, and no step makes it slower — but those five are flat, and they're named here because the audit found them.
  • The races above were simulated, not played by humans. Scripted driving lines (GOOD, PERFECT, SLOPPY) ran the game's own physics with fixed seeds, so the numbers are repeatable and checkable. One artifact to know about: the scripted SLOPPY line held early gears longer than any human would, so its losing margins at the two slowest builds read larger than a real player's would. The result it proves — sloppy driving loses, 0.000 — is the part that reflects the game. The human check is the owner's play, above.
  • Not yet checked: a purchase by a real buyer. We never fake a sale, so the first genuine checkout is the end-to-end proof of the payment step itself. Everything up to that step — this page, the game, the price — is published here, and the Fix-It Guarantee below covers the rest.
