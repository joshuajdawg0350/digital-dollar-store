# Free Wallpapers — Sources & License Basis

Built 2026-11-18 for the Digital Dollar Store free wallpapers section.
Every image here is one we may lawfully give away. Per-image source and
license are recorded in `manifest.json` (title, source, license, creator).

## Batches

- **NASA Image Library** (images.nasa.gov) — 219 wallpapers in Space.
  NASA imagery is public domain (U.S. federal government works).
- **Wikimedia Commons** — 252 wallpapers across Mountains & Nature, Ocean,
  Wolves & Wild Animals, Dragons & Fantasy, Abstract, Muscle Cars & Bikes.
  Filtered at download time to **CC0 and Public Domain only** (license read
  from each file's Commons metadata; anything else was skipped).
- **J-Dawgs in-house generation** — 53 wallpapers: all of Hustle City (14),
  Futuristic Cities (8), Cyborgs (6), Neon & Cyber (4), plus generated pieces
  in AI & Robots, Dragons & Fantasy and Abstract, and a few earlier in-house
  generated images (Charger, Midas robot). Original art generated by
  J-Dawgs Industries LLC's own media pipeline — rights-clean by construction.
  No images were taken from the paid theme kits or paid game files.

## Notes

- Openverse was attempted first for CC0/PD pulls; its API now requires
  OAuth (401 for anonymous calls), so Wikimedia Commons was used directly.
- All images were quality-filtered (resolution, sharpness, brightness) and
  cropped to 1080×2400 WebP for phone screens; thumbnails are 360×800 WebP.
- Joshua pre-approved the full batch on 2026-11-18 and will prune by name;
  removals are pulled on his word.

- **Batch 2 — J-Dawgs in-house generation (2026-11-18)** — 240 wallpapers:
  Electric Solids (80, procedural solid-color fields in the house electric
  palette), Aurora Gradients (36), Chrome & Carbon (28), Geometry & Lines (42),
  Marble & Ink (20) — all procedural originals generated with our own Python/PIL
  pipeline — plus the Hero Collection (20) and a fully restocked Muscle Cars &
  Bikes set (14), AI-generated in-house, original unbranded vehicle designs.
  Batch-2 manifest hashes use a standard 128-bit dhash.
- **Muscle Cars & Bikes swap (Joshua's order, 2026-11-18):** the previous 5
  cars entries (2 in-house Charger renders, 2 mislabeled Wikimedia pulls,
  1 Wikimedia CC0 photo) were removed entirely and replaced by the 14-piece
  in-house set above.

- **Batch 3 — J-Dawgs in-house generation (2026-11-19)** — 229 wallpapers in
  three new dark categories: Skulls (85), Gothic Whimsy (72) and Dark & Gothic
  (72). All original art generated by J-Dawgs Industries LLC's own media pipeline —
  no stock, no third-party art, no copyrighted characters or designs; pieces
  that came out resembling a known character or signature design were culled
  and regenerated before inclusion (5 generations culled on that rule).
  Processed to the section standard (1080x2400 full, 360x800 thumbs, WebP)
  with 128-bit dhash entries appended to the manifest.
