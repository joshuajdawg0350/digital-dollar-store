How to set a wallpaper

Android: 1. Tap a wallpaper you like, then tap Download. 2. Open the picture in your Gallery or Photos app. 3. Tap the menu (three dots) or Share, choose “Set as wallpaper,” pick Home screen, Lock screen, or both, then tap Set.

iPhone: 1. Tap a wallpaper you like, then tap Download. 2. Open it in the Photos app. 3. Tap the Share button, then “Use as Wallpaper,” adjust it if you like, and tap Add.
