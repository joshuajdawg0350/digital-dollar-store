HOW TO PLAY — the plain-words version
  • Your team: you and Ace (across from you) play against Rex and Slim. First team to 500 points wins the match.
  • Bidding: look at your 13 cards and bid how many tricks (rounds of 4 cards) you think you can win. Your bid and Ace's bid add together — that's your team's promise.
  • Playing a trick: tap one of your cards to play it. You must follow the suit that was led if you have it.
  • Spades are trump: a spade beats any other suit — but you can't lead a spade until spades are "broken" (someone has trumped in) or you only have spades left.
  • Scoring: make your team's promise and you get 10 points per promised trick. Every extra trick is a "bag" worth 1 point — but 10 bags cost you 100 points, so don't get greedy. Miss your promise and you lose 10 per promised trick.
  • Nil: feeling brave? Bid Nil (the 0 button) — win 100 points if you take no tricks at all, lose 100 if you take even one.
  • Your cards: this game plays with the chrome Hustle City Deck — big corner numbers, glowing suits. Tap a card once to play it; watch the score up top.
