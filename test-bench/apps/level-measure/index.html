How to use True Level & Measure

  1. Tap “Enable motion sensor.” If your phone asks for permission, tap Allow. On some iPhones this tap is required before the level can move.
  2. Lay the phone flat on the surface. Watch the round bubble — when it sits inside the blue ring, the surface is level.
  3. Read Tilt X, Tilt Y, and Angle under the circle. 0.0° means flat.
  4. Use the two bar levels when you hold the phone on its edge against a shelf or picture frame.
  5. Tap “Set zero here” to zero the level on a slanted surface you want to match.
  6. No sensor? Demo mode is on — drag the Demo tilt sliders and watch the bubbles move.
  7. Ruler: tap Centimeters or Inches, hold your object to the screen ruler, and use the Calibration slider once so the screen matches a real ruler.
