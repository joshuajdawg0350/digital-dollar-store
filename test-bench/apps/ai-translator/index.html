What this does

You talk the way you naturally talk — rough, quick, half-finished. The AI Translator turns those words into a complete, polished prompt built with the J-Dawgs Prompt Generator method, ready to paste into any AI — Muse, ChatGPT, an image or video maker, any of them. The recording, your exact words, and the finished prompt stay together in your Library, so you can always see the before and the after.

How to use it

  1. On the Record tab, tap the big round button and just say what you want to make — “make a video of my buddy launching his boat,” “write an email to my landlord,” anything. The first time, your browser asks permission for the microphone — tap Allow.
  2. Your words appear in the transcript box as you talk. Tap Stop & Save when you're done. No microphone, or the words didn't come out right? Type or paste your command in the same box — or tap Upload Audio to use a recording you already have — and type the words under it. The Translator works exactly the same.
  3. Tap Translate My Words. On the Fields tab you'll see every field the Prompt Generator works with — all open, nothing hidden. What you actually said is filled in and marked FROM YOUR WORDS. Check it and fix anything that's wrong.
  4. Anything you didn't mention shows up as a QUESTION. Answer it by typing (or tap the mic on that question, where your browser offers one) — or tap Skip, and that field is left out of your prompt entirely. The Translator never fills a field with a guess.
  5. Tap Make My Prompt. Your finished prompt appears big and readable. Tap Copy for Your AI and paste it into your AI chat — or download it as a text file.

Your Library

  • Every translation is saved with its recording, your original words, the fields, and the finished prompt.
  • Copy for Your AI copies a plain-text list of everything — words and prompts included.
  • JSON downloads a machine-readable manifest your AI can read directly.
  • All (.zip) packages the sound files, a transcripts-and-prompts file, and the JSON manifest in one download.
  • Delete — tap Delete, then tap once more to confirm. Deleted items are gone for good.

Good to know

  • Everything lives only in this browser on this device. If you clear your browser's site data, your library is erased — download anything important first.
  • About live word-capture: when your browser offers it, the words in the transcript box are captured by your browser's own speech service (in Chrome, that's Google's) — that's the browser's feature, not this app uploading anything. If you'd rather nothing leave the device at all, type or paste your words instead; the translation step itself runs entirely on your device.
  • The translation is built on-device from your words and the Prompt Generator's method — no account, no server, no waiting.
  • Works on phones, tablets, and computers — any modern browser.
