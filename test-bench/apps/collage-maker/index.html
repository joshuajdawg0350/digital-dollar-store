How to use Collage Maker

  1. Tap Add Photos and pick 2 to 9 pictures.
  2. Pick a layout, such as 2 across or 3 by 3.
  3. Tap one photo, then tap a second photo, to swap them.
  4. Slide the border width, pick a border color, and set corner roundness.
  5. Tap Download PNG or JPEG to save one finished picture.
  6. Everything stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
