How to use Quick Invoice

  1. Check the invoice number and dates at the top.
  2. Type your business details and your client's details.
  3. For each charge, type what it was, how many, and the price each. The line total figures itself out.
  4. Tap + Add Line for more charges. Tap the X on a line to remove it.
  5. Add a tax percent or a dollar discount if you need them. The total updates as you type.
  6. Tap Save Invoice to keep it on this device. Saved invoices are listed at the bottom — tap Load to work on one again, or Delete to remove it.
  7. Tap Print / Save as PDF for a clean copy to hand to your client. On most phones, pick "Save as PDF" in the print screen.

Invoices are stored only on this device. Clearing your browser data removes them.
