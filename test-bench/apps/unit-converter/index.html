How to use Convert All

  1. Tap a category at the top — Length, Weight, Temperature, Volume, Area, Speed, Time, or Currency.
  2. Type the amount in the Amount box.
  3. Pick the unit you have in “From” and the unit you want in “To.”
  4. Read the big result. It updates as you type.
  5. Tap the swap button in the middle to flip From and To.
  6. Tap “Save as favorite” to pin a pair you use often. Tap a favorite to use it again.
  7. For Currency: scroll down to see or edit the built-in rates. Rates are dated November 18, 2026 and are estimates only.
