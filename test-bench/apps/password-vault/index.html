How to use My Vault

  1. The first time, create a master password and tap Create Vault. Remember it — there is no reset and no recovery.
  2. Type a site name, username, and password, then tap Save Login.
  3. Tap Copy next to any login to copy its password, then paste it where you sign in.
  4. Tap Edit to change a login, or Delete to remove it.
  5. Use the Password Maker to create a strong password. It fills the password box for you.
  6. Tap Lock Vault when you are done. Your logins disappear from the screen until you open the vault again.

Where your data lives: only on this device, locked with strong encryption (AES-GCM) using your master password. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no account. If you forget your master password, the vault cannot be opened — nobody can recover it, including us. Clearing your browser data deletes the vault.
