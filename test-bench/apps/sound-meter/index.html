How to use Sound Check Meter

Estimate only — phone microphones are not calibrated measurement devices.

  1. Tap “Start microphone.” If your browser asks, tap Allow.
  2. Hold the phone near the sound. The big number is the current level estimate in decibels (dB).
  3. Peak shows the loudest moment and holds it. Average, Min, and Max update while you listen.
  4. The graph shows the last stretch of sound so you can see it rise and fall.
  5. Tap “Reset stats” to start the numbers over. Tap Start again to stop.
  6. If the mic is blocked, you will see a plain message here — check your browser’s site settings and allow the microphone, then try again.
