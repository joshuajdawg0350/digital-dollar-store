How to use Mirror & Magnifier

  1. Tap Turn On Mirror Camera and allow the camera when asked.
  2. Hold the phone up like a mirror. The view is flipped, like a real mirror.
  3. Move the Zoom slider to magnify small print, labels, or details.
  4. In dim light, move Brightness boost to the right.
  5. Tap Freeze Frame to hold a still picture steady so you can read it.
  6. Tap Live View to go back to the moving camera.
  7. No camera? Tap Use a Photo Instead and pick any picture, then zoom into it.
  8. If the camera is blocked, allow Camera in your browser site settings, then reload.
