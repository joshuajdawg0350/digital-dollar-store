How to use Flash Study

  1. Pick a deck, or type a name and tap Create deck.
  2. Add cards with a front and a back.
  3. Tap Study to start. Tap the card to flip it.
  4. Tap Know if you got it, or Don't Know if you missed it. Missed cards come back sooner.
  5. Tap Shuffle to mix the order. Stats show how many you know.
