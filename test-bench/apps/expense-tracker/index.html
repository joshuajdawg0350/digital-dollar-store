How to use Simple Spending

  1. Pick Expense or Income.
  2. Type the amount and pick a category.
  3. Pick the date and add a short note if you want.
  4. Tap Add entry. Totals and the chart update right away.
  5. Use the Month box at the top to see another month.
  6. Tap Export CSV to download a spreadsheet file.
