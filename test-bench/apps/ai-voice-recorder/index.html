What this does

Talk into your phone or computer, and this app keeps your voice recordings safe in one place. Then you can hand any recording to your AI assistant — Muse, ChatGPT, or any other — so it can listen, take notes, write things down for you, or remember how you say things.

How to record

  1. Tap the Record tab at the top.
  2. Tap the big round button. The first time, your browser will ask permission to use the microphone — tap Allow.
  3. Talk like you're leaving a voicemail. The blue bar shows how loud your voice is — keep it moving, not pinned to the right.
  4. Need a break? Tap Pause, then tap it again to keep going.
  5. Tap Stop & Save. Your recording is saved automatically.

How to give a recording to your AI

  1. Open the My Recordings tab.
  2. Tap Download under the recording you want to share. The sound file saves to your device.
  3. Open your AI app and attach that file, the same way you'd attach a photo.
  4. Want to hand over everything at once? Tap Download All (.zip) — you get one package with every recording plus a list file inside.
  5. Tap Copy for Your AI to copy a neat written list of your recordings (names, dates, lengths). Paste it to your AI so it knows what each recording is.

Managing your recordings

  • Play — listen before you send it.
  • Rename — give it a name your AI will understand, like “Ideas for the store” or “Letter to Mom.”
  • Delete — tap Delete, then tap once more to confirm. Deleted recordings are gone for good.

Good to know

  • Recordings live only in this browser on this device. If you clear your browser's site data, they will be erased — download anything important first.
  • Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your voice never leaves your device unless you download and share a file.
  • Works on phones, tablets, and computers — any modern browser.
