How to use QuickFix

  1. Tap Open Photo and pick a picture. On phones you can also take a new photo.
  2. To crop, pick a shape, drag the blue box, then tap Apply Crop.
  3. Use Rotate and Flip to fix sideways photos.
  4. Move the Brightness, Contrast and Saturation sliders until it looks right.
  5. Tap a filter name to try a look. Tap Original to remove it.
  6. Type words and tap Add Text to label your photo.
  7. Set the width if you want a smaller file, then tap Download PNG or JPEG.
  8. Everything happens on your device. No photo is uploaded anywhere.
