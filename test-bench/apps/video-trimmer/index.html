How to use Trim & GIF

  1. Tap Open Video and pick a video, or tap Make Sample Video to try it.
  2. Move the Start slider to where the good part begins.
  3. Move the End slider to where it should stop.
  4. Tap Play Trimmed Part Only to watch just that part.
  5. Tap Rotate if the video is sideways. Tap Mute to silence it.
  6. Pause on a moment you like and tap Save Current Frame as PNG.
  7. For a GIF, keep the trimmed part to 5 seconds or less, then tap Export GIF and wait for the download.
  8. Everything happens on your device. No video is uploaded.
