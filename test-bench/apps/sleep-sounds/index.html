How to use Sleep Sounds

  1. Tap Play on a sound to start it.
  2. Add a second or third sound to mix them together.
  3. Use each card's slider to set how loud that sound is.
  4. Use Master volume for the overall loudness.
  5. Pick a sleep timer if you want the sounds to fade out and stop on their own.
  6. Tap Stop on a sound to turn just that one off.

All sounds are created live on this device. There are no recordings and no downloads. You can mix up to 3 sounds at once.
