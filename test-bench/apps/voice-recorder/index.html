How to use Clear Voice Recorder

  1. Tap the big red Record button.
  2. Allow the microphone if your browser asks.
  3. Speak. The timer shows how long you have recorded.
  4. Tap Pause to pause, and Pause again (Resume) to keep going, if your browser supports it.
  5. Tap Stop & Save. Your recording appears in the list.
  6. Press play to listen. Drag the progress bar to jump around.
  7. Tap Rename to change a name, Download to save the file, or Delete to remove it.
  8. If the mic is denied, allow Microphone in your browser site settings, or use your phone's recorder app instead.
