How to use Streaks & Habits

  1. Type a habit name and tap the days it applies to. All days starts selected, which means daily.
  2. Tap Add Habit.
  3. Each day you do the habit, tap its big check button.
  4. Your current streak counts the scheduled days in a row you have done, counting back from today. Days the habit is not scheduled do not break a streak.
  5. The dots show the last 12 weeks: blue means done, dark means missed, dim means not scheduled.
  6. Use Edit to change a habit or Delete to remove it. Everything is saved only on this device.
