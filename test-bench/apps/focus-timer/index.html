    How to use Focus Timer

    1. Type what you are working on, if you want it in your log.
    2. Tap Start. The big clock counts down your work time.
    3. Tap Pause any time; tap Start again to keep going.
    4. When time runs out, a soft chime plays and your session is added to today's log.
    5. Take the break, then start the next work session.
    6. Change the lengths at the bottom and tap Save lengths if the standard times do not fit you.
