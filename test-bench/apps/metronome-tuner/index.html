How to use Tempo & Tune

  1. For the metronome, slide to your speed or tap Tap Tempo along with the beat.
  2. Pick how many beats are in each bar. The first beat of each bar clicks louder.
  3. Tap Start and follow the clicks and the flashing circle.
  4. For the tuner, tap Tuner, then Start Tuner, and allow the microphone.
  5. Play one note at a time. The big letter is the nearest note. The bar shows sharp (right) or flat (left). Center means in tune.
  6. Or tap a reference tone to hear the correct pitch and tune by ear.
