Funny Maps — Character Theme Songs

Six new character themes, one for everybody who didn't have a song yet. Every one is an original instrumental, written and played for Funny Maps — no vocals, no samples, nothing borrowed. Each character gets the full theme plus the exact 5-second sting that would play in the app. Nothing here is wired into the app yet — these are for your verdict first.
