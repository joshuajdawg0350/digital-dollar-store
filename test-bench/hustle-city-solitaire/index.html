📖 How to play — tap to open

The goal: build all four A-piles (top right) from Ace up to King, one suit per pile. When all 52 cards are home, you win.

The seven stacks: stack cards going down — King, Queen, Jack… — taking turns red, black, red, black. Only a King can sit on an empty space.

The deck (top left): tap it to flip cards onto the spare pile. Use the top spare card anywhere it fits. Run out? Tap the empty deck spot to shuffle the spares back in.

Moving cards: tap a card, then tap where it should go. Or drag it with your finger. Double-tap a card and it will jump home to its A-pile if it can.

Stuck on a face-down card? Move the cards on top of it away and it flips over for you.

Auto-Finish: when every card is face-up, the ⚡ button lights up — tap it and the game stacks everything home for you.

Made a mistake? Tap ↩ Undo. No penalty, no fuss.
