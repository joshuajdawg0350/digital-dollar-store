HOW TO PLAY

  • BET ONE steps your bet up per line (×20 lines). MAX BET jumps straight to your current max.
  • MAX BET GROWS: the more credits you hold, the higher your max bet climbs — balance 2,500+ unlocks 10/line, 5,000+ unlocks 25/line, 10,000+ unlocks 50/line, 25,000+ unlocks 100/line. Once a tier unlocks it stays unlocked for this game, even if your balance dips.
  • Spin two ways: press SPIN, or grab the PULL ARM on the side of the machine and pull it down — a tap on the arm works too.
  • STOP: while the reels are turning, SPIN becomes STOP — tap it and the reels land right away on the stops they were dealt. Same result, no waiting.
  • Wins pay left to right on 20 lines. Only the highest win per line pays.
  • WILD substitutes for every symbol except SCATTER.
  • WIN ×2 / WIN ×3: a multiplier anywhere on a winning line multiplies that line's win — ×2 doubles it, ×3 triples it. If two multipliers land on the same line, they multiply together (×2 and ×3 pay ×6).
  • No blank stops: every reel position is a real symbol — all 15 windows show a symbol on every spin.
  • POKER CHIPS pay from just 2 on a line.
  • 3+ SCATTER anywhere pays 2× / 7× / 35× your total bet and awards 7 / 10 / 15 FREE SPINS — line wins pay double in free spins, and free spins can retrigger.
  • SKULL VAULT — OR THE BONUS WHEEL: BONUS tokens ride reels 2, 3 and 4. When all three windows show one, the bonus opens — half the time the BONUS WHEEL spins instead of the vault: 12 wedges, every wedge pays × your total bet (MINI 4× · MINOR 8× · MAJOR 12× · GRAND 25×). The rest of the time the Skull Vault opens: twelve riveted doors — nine hide blue-chip prizes worth × your total bet (1×, 1×, 1×, 1×, 2×, 2×, 2×, 3× and one big 25× door — 38× in all) and three hide SKULLS. Pick doors one at a time; after any safe door you can STOP & BANK what you've collected. Open a skull and everything unbanked is gone. Clear all nine chip doors to bank the full 38×. The vault lands about once every 69 spins — a feature (vault or free spins) about once every 35.
  • Credits are saved on this device. Run out? Tap BUY BACK IN for a fresh start with 1,000 credits.
