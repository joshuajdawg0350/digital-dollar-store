PAYTABLE — PER LINE BET

Line wins pay left to right on 20 lines. Only the highest win per line pays. Scatter pays any position and triggers free spins with line wins doubled. Theoretical return to player: 94.6%.

HOW TO PLAY

Set your bet: BET ONE raises your bet per line (1–5 credits); every spin plays all 20 lines, so total bet is 20× your per-line bet. MAX BET jumps to 5 per line.

Spin: Press SPIN and the reels stop one by one, left to right. Matching symbols on a payline, starting from the left reel, pay per the paytable. WILD substitutes for every symbol except SCATTER.

Free spins: 3 or more SCATTER symbols anywhere award 7, 10 or 15 free spins. During free spins all line wins are doubled, and scatters can retrigger more free spins.

Credits: You start with 1,000 play credits. If you run low, tap +1,000 FREE CREDITS. Play credits only — no cash value, no prizes.
