HOW TO PLAY

  • −25 / +25 steps your total bet up or down in $25 steps (25 to start). MAX BET jumps straight to the biggest step you've unlocked.
  • Your bet ladder grows with your balance: a new $25 step unlocks every time your balance reaches it — 25 credits a spin to start, all the way up to 2,500 a spin. Once a step unlocks it stays unlocked — it never drops back when your balance dips. Starting a NEW GAME resets the ladder to 25.
  • While the reels are spinning, SPIN becomes STOP — tap it and every reel lands on its stop at once.
  • Spin two ways: press SPIN, or grab the PULL ARM on the side of the machine and pull it down — a tap on the arm works too.
  • Wins pay left to right on 20 lines. Only the highest win per line pays.
  • WILD substitutes for every symbol except SCATTER.
  • WIN ×2 / WIN ×3: a multiplier anywhere on a winning line multiplies that line's win — ×2 doubles it, ×3 triples it. If two multipliers land on the same line, they multiply together (×2 and ×3 pay ×6).
  • No blank stops: every reel position is a real symbol — all 15 windows show a symbol on every spin.
  • QUEEN pays from just 2 on a line.
  • 3+ SCATTER (the silver starburst) anywhere pays 2× / 8× / 40× your total bet and awards 7 / 10 / 15 FREE SPINS — line wins pay double in free spins, and free spins can retrigger.
  • 3+ BONUS coins anywhere on a paid spin opens THE CASE FILES: 9 sealed case files, 8 hold evidence prizes (½×–4× your total bet) and 1 holds THE SNITCH. Prizes you find are banked and kept; find The Snitch and the bonus ends — unopened files are lost. Open all 8 and a 6× CASE-CLOSED BONUS is added.
  • Credits are saved on this device. Out of credits? Hit NEW GAME to start over with a fresh 1,000.
  • Art: every card, coin and case file on this machine is original artwork made for Film Noir by J-Dawgs Industries LLC. Sounds are real recordings. The celebration show is an original black-and-white clip made for this machine.
