HOW TO BOWL

1 · Line up. Drag the ball sideways to position it. Drag on the lane to set your angle — the arrows under the ball show it.

2 · Pull back & hold. Drag down on the ball and hold. The HOOK needle starts sweeping — dead centre throws straight, off to a side hooks the ball that way.

3 · Flick up to throw. A fast, long flick lofts the ball; a shorter, slower one keeps it rolling on the ground. Flick speed is your power.

The match: 5 frames against a house pro. The 5th frame plays like the 10th — strike or spare and you can throw up to three balls. 20 seconds a throw; dawdle and the throw is gone. Perfect game is 150.

Balls: switch any time between shots with the orbs below the lane. Power, Bounce and Precise balls genuinely bowl differently — there is no single best ball, only the right ball for the leave.
