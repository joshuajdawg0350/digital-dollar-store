HOW TO PLAY

  • BET ONE raises your bet 1–5 credits per line (×20 lines). MAX BET jumps to 5 per line.
  • Spin two ways: press SPIN, or grab the PULL ARM on the side of the machine and pull it down — a tap on the arm works too.
  • Can't wait for the reels? While they roll, SPIN becomes STOP — press it and every reel lands on its stop right away. Same stops, same payouts, no waiting.
  • MAX BET grows with your balance: 2,000 credits unlocks 10 a line · 5,000 unlocks 20 · 10,000 unlocks 35 · 25,000 unlocks 50. Once a tier unlocks it stays unlocked — even if your balance dips — until you start a NEW GAME. Your current max bet is shown under the buttons.
  • Wins pay left to right on 20 lines. Only the highest win per line pays.
  • WILD substitutes for every symbol except SCATTER.
  • WIN ×2 / WIN ×3: a multiplier anywhere on a winning line multiplies that line's win — ×2 doubles it, ×3 triples it. If two multipliers land on the same line, they multiply together (×2 and ×3 pay ×6).
  • No blank stops: every reel position is a real symbol — all 15 windows show a symbol on every spin.
  • TAXI pays from just 2 on a line.
  • 3+ SCATTER anywhere pays 2× / 8× / 40× your total bet and awards 7 / 10 / 15 FREE SPINS — line wins pay double in free spins, and free spins can retrigger.
  • 3+ BONUS DICE anywhere start the DICE ROLL bonus: the pot starts at 0 / 30 / 60 × your line bet for 3 / 4 / 5 dice. Every roll of the two dice adds its total × 3 × line bet to the pot — but roll a 7 and the pot busts to zero. After any safe roll you choose: BANK IT and keep the pot, or ROLL AGAIN and push your luck.
  • Credits are saved on this device. Run out of credits — or drop below your bet — and a NEW GAME button appears: it starts you over with 1,000 fresh credits (and resets the max-bet ladder).
