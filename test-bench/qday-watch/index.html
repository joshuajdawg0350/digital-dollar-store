What is Q-Day?

Q-Day is the day a quantum computer gets strong enough to break the encryption that protects bank logins, secure websites, and private files. Nobody knows the exact date — so the smart move is getting ready before it arrives, and this page tracks the hardware getting closer, from our own bench.

Toy Shor run — factoring 15 Toy

Shor’s algorithm is the method a big quantum computer would use against today’s encryption. Our toy version of it factored 15 into 3 × 5 — a number a pocket calculator can handle.

That proves the method works end to end on our bench. It is not a real break of anything, and we won’t pretend otherwise.

Today’s honest line: the machine holds a 4-qubit entangled state clean (over 90%); wider states form, but noise eats them. That gap is exactly what this page watches, month over month — when the clean width starts climbing, the countdown is moving.
