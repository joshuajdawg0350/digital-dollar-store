How to play

This is video poker — you against the machine. No other players, no money: just play credits.

  1. Set your bet. Tap BET ONE to add a coin (1 to 5), or MAX BET to bet 5.
  2. Tap DEAL. Five cards come out, face up.
  3. Tap the cards you want to KEEP. Kept cards light up and say HOLD. Tap again to un-keep.
  4. Tap DRAW. The cards you didn't keep are replaced with new ones.
  5. Get paid. A pair of Jacks, Queens, Kings or Aces is the smallest win. The paytable at the top shows what every hand pays — it lights up when you hit one. A Royal Flush on a 5-coin bet pays 4,000.

Run out of credits? The refill button gives you 1,000 more play credits, free — they're just for fun and have no cash value.
