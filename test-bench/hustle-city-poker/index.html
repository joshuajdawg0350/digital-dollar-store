HOW TO PLAY

  • Everybody starts with 1,000 play chips. Blinds are 10 / 20.
  • You get 2 cards. Five shared cards come out in the middle: the flop (3), the turn (1), the river (1).
  • Make the best 5-card hand you can. Best hand at the showdown wins the pot.
  • Fold = give up the hand. Check = pass when nobody has bet. Call = match the bet. Raise = bet more.
  • If your chips hit zero, you can buy back in for 1,000 more play chips — free, they're play chips.
  • Hand ranks, best first: straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, one pair, high card.

Play chips only — no real money is used, won, or lost in this game.
