HOW TO PLAY

  • BET ONE raises your bet 1 credit per line at a time (×20 lines), up to your current max bet. MAX BET jumps straight to your max.
  • Your max bet grows with your balance: 100 credits a spin to start, then 200 at 2,500 credits, 300 at 5,000, 500 at 10,000 and 1,000 at 25,000. Once a tier unlocks it stays unlocked — it never drops back when your balance dips. Starting a NEW GAME resets it to 100.
  • While the reels are spinning, SPIN becomes STOP — tap it and every reel lands on its stop at once.
  • Spin two ways: press SPIN, or grab the PULL ARM on the side of the machine and pull it down — a tap on the arm works too.
  • Wins pay left to right on 20 lines. Only the highest win per line pays.
  • WILD substitutes for every symbol except SCATTER.
  • WIN ×2 / WIN ×3: a multiplier anywhere on a winning line multiplies that line's win — ×2 doubles it, ×3 triples it. If two multipliers land on the same line, they multiply together (×2 and ×3 pay ×6).
  • No blank stops: every reel position is a real symbol — all 15 windows show a symbol on every spin.
  • CHERRY pays from just 2 on a line.
  • 3+ SCATTER anywhere pays 2.4× / 9.6× / 48× your total bet and awards 7 / 10 / 15 FREE SPINS — line wins pay double in free spins, and free spins can retrigger.
  • 3+ BONUS garage doors anywhere on a paid spin opens PICK-A-GARAGE: 9 cars — pop their trunks. 8 trunks hide prizes (0.6×–4.8× your total bet) and 1 hides SCOTTY. Prizes you find are banked and kept; find SCOTTY and the bonus ends — un-popped trunks are lost. Pop all 8 and a 7.2× GARAGE BONUS is added. Half the time the BONUS WHEEL opens instead: 12 wedges, every wedge pays × your total bet — MINI 4× · MINOR 8× · MAJOR 15× · GRAND 30×.
