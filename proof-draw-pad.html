Proof, not promises

How Draw Pad was tested.

Our rule: if it's for sale, it has to work — and there has to be proof it works. So before Draw Pad went on the shelf, a scripted hand ran the exact page buyers get in a real browser at two real phone sizes — 390×844 and 360×740 — and drew on it the way a person does: real pointer drags and a real touch drag, pixels counted after every stroke. Then the store's owner drew on it himself, on his own phone, and gave the word to sell it. Below is the actual record from November 18, 2026.

The headline numbers

18 of 18 checks passed. 0 failures.

The honest limits — read these before you buy

  • The scripted runs are a browser, not a pocket. The 18 checks above ran in a desktop-class Chromium browser sized like a phone. The owner's real-phone test covers the gap a rig can't — but we have not drawn on every phone model there is.
  • Your drawing lives on your device. Autosave uses your browser's own storage on that device and that browser. Clear your browser's site data and the saved drawing goes with it — that's how a no-account app has to work, and we'd rather say it plainly.
  • Not yet checked: a purchase by a real buyer. We never fake a sale, so the first genuine checkout is the end-to-end proof of the payment step itself. Everything up to that step — this page, the app, the price — is verified and published here, and the Fix-It Guarantee below covers the rest.
