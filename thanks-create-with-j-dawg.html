How to use it — the basics in 4 steps

Step 1 — Pick a color

  1. Tap any color in the bottom bar. Tap 🎨 All Colors for the full Electric Color Chart — 66 colors picked to pop — or the rainbow square to mix any color.

Step 2 — Pick a tool

  1. Tap a tool in the top row: Pen, Pencil, Marker, Brush, Spray, Calligraphy, Eraser, Fill, Line, Square, Circle, Words, or Dropper.
  2. The sliders set brush Size, Fade, and Smooth — Smooth steadies a shaky hand.

Step 3 — Draw

  • Drag your finger (or mouse) on the picture. Mirror draws both sides at once; tap again for 4-way kaleidoscope.
  • Templates put coloring pages, comic panels and tracing sheets under your drawing. Layers stack clear sheets so you can draw on one without touching the others.
  • Undo and Redo are the first two buttons up top. The ? button explains everything again, right inside the app.

Step 4 — Save your picture

  1. Tap the big blue 💾 SAVE PICTURE button at the top — the finished picture lands in your downloads / photos.
  2. Your work also saves itself as you go. Close the page and come back, it's still here. Nothing you draw ever leaves your device.
