Two honest notes before you play

Solitaire moves by tapping. Tap a card to pick it up, tap where it goes — there is no dragging in this version, on purpose: dragging fought with the tapping on stacked piles, so we shipped the way that works every time.

Not every Solitaire deal can be won. That's true of Klondike everywhere — some deals are simply impossible. If one beats you, deal a fresh one. And the full record of how these games were tested before they went on the shelf is on our proof page — the passes and the limits, both.

Start playing — three steps

Step 1 — Open the game shelf

  1. Tap the play button above.
  2. You'll see four big buttons: Chess, Checkers, Solitaire, Crosswords.

Step 2 — Or keep the offline copy (optional)

  1. Tap the zip download link under the play button and save the zip.
  2. Open it: tap it in the Files app (iPhone) or My Files / Files by Google (Android), or double-click it on a computer. It opens into a folder named classic-games-pack.
  3. Open the folder and tap the file named index.html — the same game shelf, on your own device, no internet needed.

Step 3 — Play

  1. Tap a game and it starts. Chess and Checkers: tap one of your pieces, then tap where it goes. Solitaire: tap a card, tap the spot (double-tap sends a card to a foundation pile). Crosswords: tap a square and type.
  2. Every game has three looks — tap the skin button in the game to switch. Your pick is remembered.
  3. Your progress saves on this device automatically. Come back any time — no internet needed.

Want the whole thing on paper? The INSTRUCTIONS.txt inside the pack has every game's rules and buttons explained in plain words.
