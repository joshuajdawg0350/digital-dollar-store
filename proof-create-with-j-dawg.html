The one that failed first — told straight

First pass: drawing on the new layer at 360×740 measured 0 pixels. Re-run: 2,760 pixels. Passed.

  • On the first full run, one check failed: at the smaller phone size, after adding a layer, the test hand's drag painted nothing on the new layer.
  • We re-ran that single scenario on its own before changing anything. The cause was in the test, not the app: the Layers panel was still sliding closed over the drawing surface when the drag began, so the drag landed on the closing panel. With the panel given time to fully close — the way it is for a real person a moment later — the identical drag painted 2,760 pixels on the new layer.
  • The app was not changed to make this pass, and the first-pass failure stays in this record on purpose: a proof page that only shows passes isn't proof of anything.

The owner's own hands

Played and approved by the owner before it was shelved.

  • On November 18, 2026, Joshua — the store's owner — opened Create with J-Dawg on his own phone, drew with it, and said it works good. His word, not the test rig's, is what put it on this shelf.

The honest limits — read these before you buy

  • The scripted runs are a browser, not a pocket. The checks above ran in a desktop-class Chromium browser sized like a phone. The owner's real-phone test covers the gap a rig can't — but we have not drawn on every phone model there is, and pinch-zoom feel on a physical phone is part of what only real hands can judge.
  • Not every tool got its own measured run. The record above exercises drawing, templates, layers, the color chart, save, and help end to end. The other tools (spray, calligraphy, shapes, words, dropper, mirror) sit in the same tested build and were driven in the owner's hands-on session, but they don't each have a pixel count on this page — so we're not claiming one.
  • Your work lives on your device. Autosave uses your browser's own storage on that device and that browser. Clear your browser's site data and the saved work goes with it.
  • Not yet checked: a purchase by a real buyer. We never fake a sale, so the first genuine checkout is the end-to-end proof of the payment step itself. Everything up to that step — this page, the app, the price — is verified and published here, and the Fix-It Guarantee below covers the rest.

Verdict: PASS — 20 of 20 checks in the final record (19 first-pass, 1 re-run, documented above), November 18, 2026. If a future check finds something this pass missed, this page gets updated with what we found — including if it's bad news.
