Brand Kit + Media Kit + Rate Card
=================================

Everything a small brand or creator needs to look consistent and get hired: Brand-and-Media-Kit.pdf opens with a one-page brand board (palette with hex codes, fonts, logo do's and don'ts, voice), a print-ready business card front and back, and a three-page media kit (about, audience & numbers — real numbers only — services, testimonials, contact). Rate-Card.docx (with PDF) lists your services and prices in the same design. Fill in your real details once, in Word or any PDF editor, and send the media kit whenever a brand asks 'do you have a kit?'
