One-Page Business Plan + Business Planner Pages
===============================================

Fill One-Page-Business-Plan.docx first — eight boxes on one page: your offer, your customer, why you win, prices, costs, how customers find you, the numbers that matter, and your next three milestones. If a box is hard to fill in honestly, that's the part of the business to fix first. Business-Plan-Full.docx shows the same plan written out in full sections (market, operations, marketing, SWOT, launch checklist) using the same example business, and Business-Forecast.xlsx is the 12-month numbers page with profit and cumulative totals that calculate themselves. Everything is an EXAMPLE business — swap in yours, in Word or Google Docs.
