Pitch Deck / Company Profile Presentation (18 slides)
=====================================================

Open Pitch-Deck.pptx in PowerPoint or Google Slides: 18 widescreen slides — cover, problem, solution, product, how it works, market, traction, business model, competition, team, a financials slide with a real editable chart, roadmap, the ask, risks, contact and three appendix slides for Q&A. Every slide has speaker notes telling you what to say and what investors will ask. The whole deck is built around a fictional EXAMPLE company, BrightRoute, with made-up numbers — replace every name, figure and claim with your real ones before you show it to anyone; a pitch deck's only currency is honesty that checks out.
