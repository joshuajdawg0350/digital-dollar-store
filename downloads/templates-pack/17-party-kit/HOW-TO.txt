Party Kit — Dinosaur Birthday (original theme)
==============================================

Print Party-Kit-Dino.pdf at 100% on US Letter — card stock if you have it. Inside: a fill-in invitation and welcome sign in an original dinosaur design (footprints and a volcano — no licensed characters anywhere), two different Dino Bingo cards plus calling cards, a generated Dino Word Search with a grown-ups-only answer key, a scavenger hunt, a 'what's your dino name' chart, favour tags and a thank-you card. Fill the invitation blanks by hand or type over them in any PDF editor before printing. Everything is drawn fresh for this kit, so it's safe to use for parties you charge for, too.
