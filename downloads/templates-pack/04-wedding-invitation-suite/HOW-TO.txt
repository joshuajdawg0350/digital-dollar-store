Wedding Invitation Suite — Garden Blue (editable)
=================================================

A complete matched suite in an original garden-blue design: Invitation (5×7), Save-the-Date (5×7), RSVP card, Details card, Menu, Place Cards, Table Numbers 1–8, Welcome Sign and Thank-You Card — each a print-ready PDF at its real size, plus a Seating Chart page. Every piece carries sample wording for a sample couple; Suite-Text-Editable.docx holds every line of text in Word/Google Docs so you can swap in your names, date and venue, then print at home or hand the files to any print shop. Print invitations and save-the-dates on the heaviest matte card stock your printer takes, and cut place cards and table numbers on the lines shown.
