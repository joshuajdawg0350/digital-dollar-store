Meal Planner + Grocery List + Recipe Cards
==========================================

Print Meal-Planner.pdf at 100% on US Letter (or keep it on a tablet). Plan dinners on the weekly or monthly plan pages, build the shopping list on the aisle-by-aisle grocery page so the store trip goes in one pass, and keep the pantry and freezer inventories honest so you stop re-buying what you already own. Recipe cards hold the family favourites with ingredients, steps, servings and time, and the Meal-Prep Sunday page turns two hours of prep into five easy weeknights. Print fresh planning pages whenever you need them.
