ADHD Daily Planner (printable, low-overwhelm)
=============================================

Print ADHD-Daily-Planner.pdf at 100% on US Letter and keep a stack of Today pages ready. The design rules are the product: today has only three boxes, time is blocked in four rough chunks instead of an hourly grid, a focus-session line pairs a timer with a body double or music, and rewards are chosen in advance from the reward menu. Brain-dump pages empty your head onto paper, the weekly reset starts with what DID get done, and habits are tracked as dots — never streaks — so a missed day breaks nothing. This is a planning aid only; it makes no medical claims.
