Home Management Binder + Cleaning Schedule
==========================================

Print Home-Management-Binder.pdf at 100% on US Letter and keep it where the house runs from — kitchen counter or hallway. It holds a 20-minute daily tidy list, a weekly cleaning grid by room, monthly and room-by-room deep-clean checklists, a family chore chart, a home maintenance log (gold when you sell or claim on a warranty), emergency contacts and house facts like the water shut-off, important dates, and a one-page money snapshot. Print fresh chore charts and checklists as you use them up.
