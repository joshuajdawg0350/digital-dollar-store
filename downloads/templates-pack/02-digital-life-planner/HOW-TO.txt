Digital Life Planner — Hyperlinked + Printable
==============================================

Open Digital-Life-Planner.pdf on a tablet in GoodNotes or Notability and the Index page links jump straight to each section (the PDF also has bookmarks). Prefer paper? Print it at 100% on US Letter and print extra weekly, daily, habit and notes pages whenever you run out — page numbers for reprints are listed on the last page. Everything is undated on purpose: a year-at-a-glance for 2027, goal pages, 12 monthly pages, weekly and daily pages, habit trackers, a money page, a meal page, to-do lists, notes and a print-and-cut sticker page, so the planner never expires.
