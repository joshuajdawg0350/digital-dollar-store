Freelance Contract + Proposal + Client Onboarding Pack
======================================================

The full kit for landing and running freelance work, in Word/Google Docs format with PDFs to show the finished look: Proposal.docx wins the job (problem, plan, three packages, timeline, one clear next step), Services-Agreement.docx is the plain-language general agreement — it is a general template, NOT legal advice, so have a lawyer review it before relying on it for big projects — Onboarding-Questionnaire.docx collects everything you need the day a client signs, Welcome-Packet.docx sets expectations so projects run smooth, and Email-Scripts.docx gives you copy-and-send words for inquiries, quiet proposals and late payments. Fill in the blanks and your business details once, then reuse the pack on every job.
