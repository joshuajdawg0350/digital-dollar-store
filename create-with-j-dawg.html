How to use it — the basics in 4 steps

Step 1 — Pick a color

  1. Tap any color in the bottom bar. Tap 🎨 All Colors for the full Electric Color Chart — 66 colors in families, from Electric Blues to Metallics — or tap the rainbow square to mix any color you want.

Step 2 — Pick a tool

  1. Tap a tool in the top row: Pen, Pencil, Marker, Brush, Spray, Calligraphy, Eraser, Fill, Line, Square, Circle, Words, or the Dropper that copies any color already in your picture.
  2. The sliders under the tools set your brush Size, Fade, and Smooth — Smooth steadies a shaky hand for clean outlines.

Step 3 — Draw

  • Drag your finger (or mouse) on the picture. Mirror draws both sides at once — tap it again for 4-way kaleidoscope.
  • Templates (bottom tab) put coloring pages, comic panels, papers and tracing sheets under your drawing, so you can color, trace, or draw on top. Changing templates never erases your drawing.
  • Layers (bottom tab) are clear sheets stacked up — sketch on one, color on another, and clear or hide one without touching the rest. Tap ＋ Add a layer for another sheet.
  • Mistakes? Undo and Redo are the first two buttons in the top row. ERASE EVERYTHING wipes the whole picture — it makes you tap twice, so a slip can't wreck your work.
  • Pinch with two fingers to zoom, or slide with two fingers to move around a big picture.

Step 4 — Save your picture

  1. Tap the big blue 💾 SAVE PICTURE button at the top. The finished picture lands in your downloads / photos as a PNG.
  2. Your work also saves itself as you go — close the page and come back, it's still here.
  3. The ? button at the top explains everything again, right inside the app.
