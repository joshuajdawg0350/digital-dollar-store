The honest limits — read these before you buy

  • No Canva links. Some marketplaces sell these template types as Canva links. Ours are real files instead — print-ready PDFs, editable Word documents, and editable slide decks — because we only sell what we built and can stand behind. If you specifically need Canva, this pack is not that.
  • The Life OS item is not a Notion duplicate link. A live Notion link can't live inside a ZIP file, so item 19 ships as the full system pre-built in a spreadsheet, plus a step-by-step Notion rebuild guide and printable dashboard pages. The substitution is stated inside the pack too, in its HOW-TO and on the guide's first page.
  • Checked with desktop tools. Spreadsheets were recalculated in LibreOffice and documents rendered on a desktop. The files are standard formats (.xlsx, .docx, .pptx, .pdf) that open in Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Google Sheets/Docs/Slides, and any PDF reader — but they were not opened one by one on a phone.
  • The freelance agreement is a general template, not legal advice — it says so on its face. Have a lawyer look at any contract before you rely on it.
  • Not yet checked: a purchase by a real buyer. We never fake a sale, so the first genuine checkout is the end-to-end proof of the payment step itself. Everything up to that step — this page, the checkout price, the download file and its hash — is verified and published here, and the Fix-It Guarantee below covers the rest.
