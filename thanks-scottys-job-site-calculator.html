How to use it

Step 1 — Tap Open

  1. Tap the big Open button above. The calculator opens right in your browser — nothing to install, nothing to download — and the box above the steps shows you how to put its icon on your home screen, like a regular app.
  2. It works on phones, tablets and computers.

Step 2 — Do feet-inch-fraction math

  • Type a measurement the way you'd say it: 12 ′ 6 1/2 ″ is twelve feet, six and a half inches. Tap a fraction key (¹⁄₁₆ up to ⁷⁄₈) to add it — no decimal converting, ever.
  • Try it: 8′ + 4′ 6″, 12′ 6″ ÷ 3, or 9′ × 2. The answer shows in feet-inches-fractions, and underneath it the same number in decimal feet, decimal inches and millimeters.
  • On a keyboard it works too — numbers, ' for feet, " for inches, / for fractions, Enter for =.

Step 3 — Use the Quick Jobs tools

  • Roof — pick a pitch (3/12 to 12/12) and a run, get the common rafter length and angle.
  • Wall Framing — wall length, height and stud spacing give you the stud count, plates and drywall sheets.
  • Stairs — floor-to-floor rise in, risers, treads, total run and stringer length out, with the 2×riser + tread comfort check built in. Always verify your local code.
  • Concrete — length, width and thickness give you cubic yards and how many 60 lb or 80 lb bags, with a waste percentage so you don't come up short.
  • Board Feet — thickness × width × length × pieces, with a price per board foot if you want the lumber cost too.
  • Diagonal / Square — the 3-4-5 method for squaring a foundation or a deck: if both diagonals match, you're square.
